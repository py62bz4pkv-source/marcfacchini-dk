Clarity, calm and direction

Alongside my work as a performing artist, I find great value in making artistic experiences happen from behind the scenes. I care deeply about creating compelling cultural experiences that invite community and immersion.

Since 2009 I have developed and delivered cultural and educational formats from start to finish; from the initial idea and fundraising to production planning, volunteer management and the event itself.
